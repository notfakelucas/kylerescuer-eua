Who is Kyle?

One rescuer. One mission. 400 lives that depend on your help.

My name is Kyle, I'm an animal rescuer, and I make sure help reaches shelters caring for animals in critical condition.

Through SOS Animal Help, I visit shelters that are at their breaking point, see firsthand what rescuers are up against, and bring financial support for food, medicine, vet care and urgent needs, so these animals get the chance to keep living.

“Hunger and debt: in recent months, donations have dropped sharply and the situation has gotten worse. 22 lives have already been lost, and other animals are still sick, waiting for urgent care.”

Even facing so much hardship, I can't turn my back. If we don't help now, many of these animals will keep suffering with no help, no food and no one to look out for them.

Questions

Before you give

Is my donation secure?+−

Yes. Payments are processed by our payment providers on their own encrypted systems. We never see or store your card number.

Who receives the money?+−

SOS Animal Help, Inc., a California nonprofit public benefit corporation (EIN 41-4770760). It supports independent rescuers like Kyle and passes the donations on to the shelters.

Where exactly does the money go?+−

Straight into caring for the animals: food, vet visits and surgeries, shelter rent, medication and upkeep. The full monthly breakdown is on this page.

Is there a minimum amount?+−

$10 for a one-time gift and $5 for a monthly gift. For more than $5,000, please email us.

Can I cancel a monthly donation?+−

Yes - any time, free, no questions asked. Email support@sosanimalhelp.org, message us on WhatsApp at +1 (805) 975-8416, or cancel it yourself in PayPal. We confirm every cancellation by email within 2 business days.

Will I get a receipt?+−

Yes. A confirmation goes to the email you use at checkout, and we send a written receipt for any donation on request at support@sosanimalhelp.org.

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp or email support@sosanimalhelp.org.
