Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how SOS Animal Help, Inc. (“SOS Animal Help,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our websites and donation pages.

1. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies, such as local storage, pixels, and other tracking technologies, to operate our website, process donations, remember preferences, understand how visitors use our website, and measure the effectiveness of our campaigns.

Some of these technologies are provided directly by us, while others are provided by third-party services.

2. Types of cookies we use

Essential cookies

These technologies are necessary for the website and donation process to function properly.

They may be used to:

  • maintain website functionality;
  • process and secure donations;
  • prevent fraud and abuse;
  • maintain payment sessions; and
  • remember information required during the donation process.

Essential technologies may be used by us and by our secure payment providers.

Preference cookies

These technologies help remember choices you make when using our website, such as language, currency, location preferences, or other website settings.

Analytics and measurement technologies

We may use analytics technologies to understand how visitors interact with our website, including which pages are visited, how visitors arrive at the website, and how our donation campaigns perform.

This information helps us improve our website and fundraising campaigns.

Advertising technologies

We may use advertising and measurement technologies provided by platforms such as Meta and other advertising partners.

These technologies may help us understand whether an advertisement resulted in a website visit or donation and measure the effectiveness of our campaigns.

Third-party content

Some pages may include content or services provided by third parties, such as video players, payment services, or embedded tools.

These providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their respective privacy policies.

3. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Depending on your location and the technologies used on the website, you may also be provided with additional controls to manage optional cookies and tracking technologies.

Blocking certain essential cookies may affect the operation of the website or prevent some features, including payment functionality, from working correctly.

4. Third-party services

We may use third-party service providers for payments, analytics, advertising, video hosting, website infrastructure, security, and other services necessary to operate our websites.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how we collect, use, and share personal information, please review our Privacy Policy.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, technologies, service providers, or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated date shown above.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or our privacy practices, contact us at:

  • SOS Animal Help, Inc.
  • Email: support@sosanimalhelp.org
